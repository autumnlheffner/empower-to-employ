Internships

Internships give ETE students opportunities to apply classroom lessons, take on responsibilities, and gain confidence in real workplaces.

The Twisted Prop

At The Twisted Prop, students learn through everyday workplace responsibilities. Their experiences strengthen teamwork, communication, and customer service skills.

View Photos

Flightline Cafe

At Flightline Cafe, students gain hands-on experience while practicing customer service, communication, and teamwork in a busy work environment.

View Photos

RoboCade

At RoboCade, students explore a different work environment and practice completing tasks, working with others, and taking responsibility.

View Photos

Food Bank

At the food bank, students support their community while practicing teamwork, organization, and workplace responsibilities.

View Photos

Louise's Kitchen

At Louise’s Kitchen, students gain practical experience with customer service, communication, and teamwork while learning about daily workplace routines.

View Photos
