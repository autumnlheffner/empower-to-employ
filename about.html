About Empower to Employ

Education. Independence. Opportunity.

Empower to Employ is a two-year inclusive college program that prepares students for employment, greater independence, and life after college.

What Is Empower to Employ?

ETE allows students to experience college while developing practical skills for employment, independent living, and everyday life.

Students learn through college classes, hands-on projects, career preparation, and community experiences. These opportunities help students explore their interests, build confidence, and work toward individual goals.

What Students Learn

ETE combines academic learning with practical skills that students can use in everyday life and future workplaces.

01 / LIFE SKILLS

Independent Living

Students develop skills related to independence, self-advocacy, communication, and everyday adult life.

02 / CAMPUS LIFE

College Experience

Students participate in college classes, campus events, and activities that help them feel connected to the college community.

03 / CAREER READINESS

Personal & Workplace Skills

Students practice communication, organization, responsibility, teamwork, problem-solving, and self-advocacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Empower to Employ and the opportunities available to students.

What is Empower to Employ?

Empower to Employ (ETE) is a two-year inclusive postsecondary program at Polk State College that helps students develop employment, independence, and life skills.

What do students learn in ETE?

Students develop workplace skills, communication, independence, self-advocacy, and other important skills through college classes and hands-on experiences.

Does ETE offer internships?

Yes! Students participate in internships where they can gain real-world workplace experience. Explore Internships.

Can students earn certifications?

ETE students have opportunities to earn Florida Ready to Work credentials, including Soft Skills and Digital Skills. View Certifications.

What career opportunities can students explore?

Students can explore career interests through coursework, projects, internships, and workplace experiences. Explore Career Pathways.
